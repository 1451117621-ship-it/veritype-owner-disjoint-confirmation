# Results Text (Paper-Ready Draft)

On the preregistered owner-disjoint cohort of 188 owners (3,760 machine-validated targets), the structure-aware routing policy recovered 3,147 targets (83.70%). Its recorded incremental fallback cost was $47.86, within the matched $48.00 route cap. Relative to the confidence-only comparator, structure-aware routing improved accuracy by 1.70 percentage points (owner-clustered 95% CI 0.45 to 2.95; p = 0.012). It also exceeded the hash-random replay baseline by 1.30 percentage points (95% CI 0.30 to 2.35; p = 0.018). The difference between structure-aware routing and the full-feature ablation was not distinguishable from zero (0.11 percentage points; 95% CI -0.38 to 0.61; p = 0.74).

Interpretation: these results support the structure-aware policy relative to the prespecified confidence-only and hash-random comparators. They do not establish that adding the full feature set improves on the structure-only policy.
